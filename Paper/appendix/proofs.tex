\subsection{Realizability}
Constructive embedding of row-stochastic tables $P_g$ into a fixed-depth outcome
architecture with state slots and gate routing. Width scales as $O(K+M)$.

\subsection{Forward--backward credit}
Let $q_{t-1}$ be the forward marginal and $b_t$ the backward answer residual.
Outcome credit at step $t$ is $\mathrm{Rule}(-\nabla_{P_t}\ell_{\mathrm{out}})=
\bar q_{t-1}\bar b_t^\top/p_y$. Local process CE needs neither inference and has
constant lower bound in Frobenius norm. Mixing-ball bound:
$\|\mathrm{Rule}(-\nabla_{P_t}\ell_{\mathrm{out}})\|_F\le
\frac{1-1/K}{\alpha}\varepsilon_{\mathrm{rule}}^{D-1}$ when $p_y\ge\alpha$.

\subsection{Complete mixing}
When $P_g=U$, outcome gradients vanish on the simplex interior; process targets
recover $T_g-U$ by reversibility of the uniform chain.
