% Rebuild: python experiments/build_rule_credit_table.py

Let $\widehat P_g$ be the induced rule table at gate $g$. The theorem's credit
quantity is the Frobenius norm of the table-space gradient
$\|\mathrm{Rule}(-\nabla_{\widehat P_g}\ell)\|_F$, aggregated over gates and
probe contexts. Primary columns: \texttt{credit\_table\_mean}, \texttt{credit\_table\_max}.
Do not confuse with parameter-space pullback norms or alignment cosines.
