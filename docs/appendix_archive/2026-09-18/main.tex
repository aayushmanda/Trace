\section*{Reproducibility statement}
The artifact rebuilds reliability-sweep figures and tables from hashed archived
CSVs and numerically checks the shared transition-table model's credit and gradient identities
without training. \texttt{results/paper/ARTIFACT\_MANIFEST.md} lists, for every
table and figure, the exact source file, its SHA-256 hash, and the command
that produced it. The corrupted-trace study fits tabular rules to sampled
local pairs and rebuilds its figure from saved results. It shares gate
semantics and the local corruption law with the Transformer sweeps, but uses
uniform gate-string sampling rather than uniform gate-family sampling, a
difference checked directly and found not to explain the gap between their
recovery frontiers. The LoRA adaptation study (\cref{app:legacy-protocol})
has since been rerun in full, with raw per-seed generations, adapters, and
local-margin CSVs archived under \texttt{results/paper/smollm/artifacts/}.
The five-condition comparison (\cref{tab:supervision-comparison}), whose
original run logs were never located, has likewise been rerun in full at
its own recorded settings, with per-seed rows, configuration, and console
log archived under \texttt{results/paper/e1\_five\_condition\_rerun/}.
Neither aggregate carries a provenance gap any longer. The only open item
is the numerical gap between this rerun's clean-process point and the
separately run reliability sweep's, which \cref{sec:reliability} traces
to training-set size and seed identity for most of its size, with a
roughly four-point residual remaining unexplained.

\paragraph{Organization.}
\Cref{app:architectures} gives a short realizability sanity check
(\cref{thm:realizability}): both supervision formats admit finite-parameter
Transformer executors with zero greedy execution error, ruling out an
unrepresentable-computation explanation for the training gap without
carrying the full construction. \Cref{app:shared-kernel-embedding} then
shows the shared transition-table model itself is an explicit mechanistic
subspace of the process architecture's parameters, not an external
surrogate, up to a routing error that vanishes as the construction's
attention score grows (\cref{prop:shared-kernel-embedding}).
\Cref{app:operator-proofs} proves the
shared transition-table credit, depth-suppression, complete-mixing, and
true-rule-direction results, and \cref{app:credit-suppression} confirms
them numerically without training. \Cref{app:noise-threshold-proof} proves
the reliability threshold, and \cref{app:outcome-rescue-diagnostic} gives
the full statement, proof, and figure for the one-dimensional clean-answer
diagnostic only summarized in \cref{sec:outcome-rescue}.
\Cref{app:noise-threshold} reports finite-sample recovery together with
the corruption-structure and gate-sampling checks. \Cref{app:legacy-protocol}
gives the protocol for the five-condition comparison, the reliability
sweeps, and the LoRA adaptation study. \Cref{app:gradient-alignment} gives
the full protocol and per-transition results for the gradient-level
credit-asymmetry diagnostic summarized in \cref{sec:gradient-alignment}.
\Cref{app:other-substrates} checks the same reliability pattern on two
further task families beyond the Boolean circuit.
